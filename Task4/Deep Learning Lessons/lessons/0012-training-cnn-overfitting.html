Training a CNN & overfitting

The one skill: spot and fix overfitting on CIFAR-10.

By the end you can Explain train/val gap, dropout, data augmentation, early stopping, and regularization.

Overfitting

Model memorizes training data but fails to generalize. Look for training accuracy >> validation accuracy.

Techniques

  • Dropout(rate): Randomly turn off neurons during training → prevents co-adaptation
  • Data augmentation: Flip/rotate/crop images → more varied data
  • Early stopping: Stop when validation loss stops improving
  • BatchNormalization: Stabilizes training (sometimes helps)

CIFAR-10 notes

Color images, 10 classes, harder than MNIST. CNNs are needed; normalization to [0,1] is important.
