Generative Adversarial Networks

The one skill: understand the generator vs discriminator game.

By the end you can Explain adversarial training, min-max game, and why GANs generate data.

Idea

Two networks compete: Generator (G) tries to create fake data that look real; Discriminator (D) tries to tell real from fake.

Goal

G learns to fool D; D learns to not be fooled. At equilibrium, G produces data indistinguishable from real.

Training intuition

  1. Train D on real + fake data (binary classification)
  2. Train G to make D classify fakes as real (by backpropagating through D)
  3. Repeat

Applications

  • Image generation, super-resolution
  • Style transfer
  • Data augmentation

Challenges

  • Training instability
  • Mode collapse (G produces limited variety)
