Tensors & automatic differentiation

The one skill: know why frameworks use tensors and autodiff.

By the end you can Define tensors, explain computational graphs, and why TensorFlow/PyTorch compute gradients automatically.

What is a tensor?

A tensor is a generalization of scalars/vectors/matrices to n dimensions. In TF, everything is a tensor.

  • scalar: 0D
  • vector: 1D
  • matrix: 2D
  • ... higher dims

Computational graph

Frameworks build a graph of operations; when you call GradientTape (TF) or backward() (PyTorch), they traverse backwards applying chain rule.

Why not just NumPy?

  • GPU acceleration
  • Automatic differentiation
  • High-level APIs (layers, optimizers)
  • Efficient batching
