What hidden layers learn

The one skill: see how hidden layers build up features.

By the end you can Explain feature learning, why depth helps, and interpret the spiral/XOR examples.

Feature hierarchy

  • Early layers: simple features (edges, gradients)
  • Middle layers: combinations (shapes, textures)
  • Deep layers: complex concepts (objects, faces)

Why hidden layers work

Each layer transforms data; stacking nonlinear layers gives exponentially more expressive functions. This is why deep nets beat shallow ones on complex data.
