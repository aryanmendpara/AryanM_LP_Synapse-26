Debugging training

The one skill: diagnose common training failures from symptoms.

By the end you can Spot problems from loss/accuracy curves and fix init, learning rate, scaling issues.

Common issues

SymptomLikely causeFix
Loss stays flat / accuracy ~randomBad init (all zeros), very small LR, or dead activationsRandom init (small), check shapes, try larger LR
Loss explodes to inf/NaNLR too big, large unscaled inputs, exploding gradientsSmaller LR, normalize/scale, gradient clipping (later)
Loss decreases then jumps upLR too high (oscillation)Lower LR or learning rate schedule
Train acc high, val/test much lowerOverfittingMore data, dropout, regularization, early stopping
Both train and val badUnderfitting (model too simple)Bigger model, more features, train longer

Initialization

Don't init all weights to zero. All neurons compute same thing, get same gradients, stay identical (symmetry). Use small random values: np.random.randn(...) * 0.01.

Learning rate

  • Too small: painfully slow, might get stuck
  • Just right: smooth loss decrease
  • Too large: diverges, oscillates

Reading curves

Plot loss vs epochs: healthy training goes down smoothly. Spikes mean LR too big. Flatline means stuck.
