Lesson 18 · Deep Learning

Transformers & attention

The one skill: understand self-attention and why "attention is all you need".

By the end you can Explain self-attention, Q/K/V, scaled dot-product attention, and why transformers replaced RNNs for many tasks.

Problem with RNNs

Sequential processing is slow; long-range dependencies still hard despite LSTMs. Can't parallelize well.

Attention idea

Instead of compressing everything into one hidden state, let each word "attend" to all other words in the sequence to decide what’s relevant.

Self-attention: Q, K, V

For each token, compute Query (Q), Key (K), Value (V). Attention weights come from similarity between Q and K.

Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) * V

Divide by sqrt(d_k) to stabilize gradients (especially for large d_k).

Why transformers?

  • Parallelizable: Process all tokens at once
  • Long-range: Direct connections between any positions
  • Scalable: Foundation for LLMs (BERT, GPT)

Key paper

Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need" (2017) — read the abstract/introduction first.
